### Energy scale for the largest gradient

The energy reward per decision is `g · exp(−c/s)`. If a gait makes every decision's energy a fraction ε cheaper, the
mean reward rises by `ε · E[g (c/s) exp(−c/s)]`; for one value of `c` that peaks at `s = c` (1/e). The per-decision `c` is
spread wide, so `tools/energy_scale_gradient.py` takes it from a stochastic rollout (`mode=eval var.stochastic=1
var.cot_out=…`, the policy sampling as in training). On `devita_eq_gail4_40m`, 376,000 settled decisions:

| `energyScale` | Mean energy reward | Gradient ε | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 378 | 0.27 | 0.313 | CoT 25th percentile |
| 482 | 0.35 | 0.329 | median |
| **536** | 0.38 | **0.331** | peak |
| 652 | 0.44 | 0.325 | 75th percentile |
| 965 | 0.57 | 0.291 | the run's scale |

The plateau runs from about 480 to 650; 965 gives 12 % less gradient. The training log's mean CoT (627) includes the
first 2 s of each episode, which the rollout leaves out. `devita_eq_gail4_es550_200m` uses 550 with everything else
as `devita_eq_gail4_40m`, for 200M steps.

## Energy

- **`energyScale`.** `tools/derive_energy_scale.py` gives 480 J/m as the median over the last half of training.
  The converged gait costs 392 J/m (mean of the last 20 summaries), and that is the value used in the recalibration
  run `devita_walk150_20m_b`. That run did not improve on 587: CoT 425 against 392 J/m, training death rate 0.37
  against 0.26 (one seed each, last 20 summaries). `training/devita_h173.yaml` now has 643, Unity's 587 rescaled for the
  corrected axes (below).
- **The energy reward of both DeVita runs was computed on wrong motor axes.** The books rebuilt the motor frame with
  Y as the plane axis and Z as the normal; Jolt's constraint space has them the other way. The twist axis was right, but
  the swing work was off: on the 587 policy the old books read 8.7 % low in gross work and 18.5 % low in negative work.
  The axes now come from `GetConstraintToBody2`, and Newton–Euler agrees with the motors to 6e-5. Both DeVita runs trained
  on the old signal and the CoT and `energyScale` figures above are from it; they were retrained (above). The flat walker
  has `energyBonusWeight 0`, so its reward was not affected.
- **S7.5 passed by accident.** Its 0.5 % came from the wrong axes. With the right ones the motors alone leave 13 %, as
  random actions slam the joint stops. The test now counts limit work through Newton–Euler and gets 0.74 %, the plan's 2 %
  gate.
- **`DeVita/*` stats were never published during training.** The system wrote them before Rewards and the reset, which
  clear `io.stats`, so neither 20M run has them in its logs. They now leave with the reset, and `mode=eval` writes
  them for every policy (the table above); the policies themselves were unaffected.
- **The motor books alone do not close on walking.** Net residual against Σ|work|: −20.0 % on the walker policy, −11.0 %
  on DeVita (tick-wise 35 % and 56 %). They leave out limit work, and contact work is taken at each segment's centre of
  mass. The rollout books (`energy-formulas.md`) use Jolt's contact impulses (patch P-C) and close to 0.04 % net.
