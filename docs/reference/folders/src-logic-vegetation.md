# src/logic/vegetation

The plants of the world on the logic side: the library of 80 SpeedTree species imported from the Unity project
(`resources/vegetation/`, generated by `tools/speedtree_import.py`, git ignored; [vegetation](../../vegetation.md)),
the `Plant` entities that `VegetationPass` draws, the forests in patches on gentle land
([forests](../../forest.md)) and the test scatterings.

## Components and context variables

- [Plant](../components/Plant.md): a plant of the library, next to a Transform
- [ChunkForest](../components/ChunkForest.md), [ChunkPlants](../components/ChunkPlants.md): a chunk's plants,

## Worth knowing

- **World-anchored**: forest layers are grids of `spacing` metres anchored to the world, one jittered candidate a
  cell, every random draw made whether the plant is kept or not, so a plant only depends on its cell and the
  parameters, never on which chunk asks or when; a plant belongs to the chunk whose cubes it stands on.
- `vegetationLibrary(reg)` emplaces the library on first use; it is empty (with a warning) when there is no
  `library.json` in `./resources/vegetation` nor in the source tree's (the build does not copy it).
- `Plant::lod`: -1 by screen height, a fixed level, or `Plant::volume` (from the species' baked 3D volume).
- Plants are sunk a little by their height and the slope, so the foot of the trunk is not in the air downhill.
