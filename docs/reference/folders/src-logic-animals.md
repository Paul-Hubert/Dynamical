# src/logic/animals

The animals of the library (`resources/animals/`, built by `tools/animal_import.py`): loading species, playing and blending their clips, the procedural walk read out of a walk clip, herds that graze over the terrain, animals that walk where a rider steers them, and the physical animals (ragdolls on Jolt driven by a policy, one per world) with the generator of their bodies.

Two families live here:

- **Kinematic animals** (host registry): an entity with `Transform` + `Animal`; `AnimalAnimation` plays its clips into an `AnimalPose`, `AnimalGait` bends and plants it, `AnimalPass` (renderer) draws it. `Herds`, `AnimalLineup`, `AnimalWalkers` and `SteeredWalk` make and move them.
- **Physical animals** (world registries, docs/physical-agents/reports/animal-agents.md): the `AnimalBot` context variable of a world, made by `AnimalFactory` from an `AnimalSpawn`, with a body file generated by `animal_body.cpp`; `AnimalBotSteer` mirrors it in the host as an `AnimalBotProxy` for riders.

## Systems registered here

Kinematic animals (`registerAnimals()`, `registerAnimalSteer()`), host scope:

- [AnimalAnimation](../systems/AnimalAnimation.md): plays the clips into `AnimalPose`.
- [AnimalGait](../systems/AnimalGait.md): the procedural walk on animals that play a gait clip.
- [Herds](../systems/Herds.md): herds anchored to cells of the world, grazing and moving on.
- [HerdWatch](../systems/HerdWatch.md): the camera round a herd.
- [AnimalLineup](../systems/AnimalLineup.md): the library in rows (scene `animals_test`).
- [AnimalWalkers](../systems/AnimalWalkers.md): animals walking paths (scene `animals_gait`).
- [SteeredWalk](../systems/SteeredWalk.md): kinematic animals steered by a rider.
- [AnimalBotSteer](../systems/AnimalBotSteer.md): the host proxy of each world's physical animal, and its command.

Physical animals (`registerAnimalAgents()`), world scope:

- [AnimalFactory](../systems/AnimalFactory.md), [AnimalDrive](../systems/AnimalDrive.md), [AnimalSync](../systems/AnimalSync.md), [AnimalTask](../systems/AnimalTask.md), [AnimalTermination](../systems/AnimalTermination.md), [AnimalRewards](../systems/AnimalRewards.md), [AnimalObservation](../systems/AnimalObservation.md), [AnimalEpisode](../systems/AnimalEpisode.md), [AnimalSoftGround](../systems/AnimalSoftGround.md), [AnimalGaitPlayer](../systems/AnimalGaitPlayer.md), [AnimalChecks](../systems/AnimalChecks.md).

## Main functions and classes

- `AnimalLibrary::load`, `species`, `loadSpecies`, `loadGeometry` (the files `library.json`, `species/<id>.json`, `.bin`, `.anim`); `animalsDirectory()`, `animalLibrary(reg)` (the context's library, made on first use).
- `AnimalSpecies::sample`, `worldMatrices`, `skinMatrices`; `blend()`; `Animal::play(clip, seconds)` (cross fade); `evaluate()`.
- `analyseGait()`: the legs, their chains, stance phases, soles, swing curves, hips and speed of a walk clip, with no knowledge of the animal (39 of 51 walkers of the library). `proceduralWalk()`: the pose of a walking animal from its motion, pure but for its `AnimalGait`. `GaitStyle::parse`, `names`, `values`, `json` (17 parameters).
- `stepWalker()`, `walkerCurvature()`, `levelGround()`, `headingOf()`.
- `HerdDefinition::parse`, `HerdSys::describe`, `HerdSys::nearest` (herds are a pure function of the definition, the seed and the land).
- `writeGaitDemos()`, `GaitRanges::parse`, `drawCommand` / `stepCommand`, `GaitWalk`.
- `generateAnimalBody()`, `loadAnimalBody()`, `animalBodyBind()`, `segmentPoses()`, `skeletonFromSegments()`.
- `buildAnimalRagdoll()`, `standAnimalBot()`, `driveAnimalBot()`, `syncAnimalBot()`, `observeAnimal()`, `rateAnimal()`.

## Dependencies

- `logic/agents`: `GroundField`, `TerrainField`, `PlaneField` (the ground the animals stand on), the ragdoll build, `AgentIO`, environment parameters, `WalkRoutes`, the human walker's assets.
- `logic/terrain`: `TerrainGenerator` (heightfield, densities), `MarchingCubes` (for `TerrainField`).
- `logic/vegetation`: `ForestField` (cover for the herds' `where`), `VegetationLibrary` and `Plant` (trunks to avoid, crowns for `HerdWatch`).
- `logic/components`: `Transform`, `Camera`, `ModelRef`; `logic/systems`: `System`.
- `physics`: Jolt world, terrain colliders, `FixedSteps` (physical animals).
- `scene`: the system and component registries, `Worlds` / `World`, the scenario runner.
- `util`: log, paths, profile zones. Taskflow (pose batches).
- Used by `renderer/passes/animal_pass.*` (draws `Animal` + `AnimalPose`, writes `AnimalStats`), `view/` (debug menu, captures, `CameraWalk`, `AnimalAgentView`), `logic/player/mount.*` (riding), `logic/sandbox`.
